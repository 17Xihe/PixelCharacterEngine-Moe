# Character Proportion


The character must have obvious chibi proportions.


Head:

40%


Body:

35%


Legs:

25%



Characteristics:


- oversized cute head
- small compact body
- short limbs
- tiny hands and feet
- rounded silhouette


The character should feel like:

a cute collectible game mascot.


Avoid:

- realistic human proportions
- tall body
- realistic anatomy
